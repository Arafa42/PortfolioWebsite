Research & Focuscurrent work

General World Models

2025 - 2027

This project provides companies with inspiration, tools, and strategic knowledge to integrate General World Models responsibly and efficiently into their workflows. We explore how generative AI models can be leveraged for practical enterprise applications.

HEART: Game Development Toolkit

2025 - 2027

A Unity-based toolkit that integrates game analytics and psychophysiological data for real-time UX insights. This research focuses on monitoring Human Experience Analytics in Real-Time during interactive experiences and gaming.

Promptable AI-Camera (PAIC)

2024 - 2026

An innovative AI-driven camera system that enables real-time adaptation of livestreams through natural language prompts. This research explores the intersection of AI, computer vision, and live media production.

Storyliving in Extended Reality

2024 - 2026

Research into storytelling techniques and parameters to enhance immersion and sense of presence in Extended Reality (VR/AR/MR). This project investigates how narrative design can maximize user engagement in immersive environments.

Smart Contract Local Energy Community

2022 - 2025

This project explores blockchain technology for the virtual exchange of energy within local energy communities. We develop smart contract solutions to enable transparent and decentralized peer-to-peer energy trading.
